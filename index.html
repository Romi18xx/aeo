Three moves that close the gap fastest

Publish a structured FAQ

Answer engines lift FAQ and definition blocks almost verbatim. Add schema-marked Q&A blocks to your ten highest-intent pages first.

Tighten entity clarity

Make sure your about page, footer, and structured data all agree on one canonical description of who you are and what you do.

Earn third-party citations

LLMs weight independent sources heavily. Prioritize placements on the outlets these models already cite most in your category.
